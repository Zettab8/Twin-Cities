About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Story

Let's help animals find their forever homes! Our shelter was founded by a family that grew up with all kinds of animals on a farm. We are passionate about keeping animals safe and helping them find their forever homes.

Our Core Values

Taking Initiative

If you see something that needs attention, speak up or take action.

Transparency

We share monthly reports so our community can see what is happening at the shelter.

Love Animals

Loving and respecting animals is at the heart of what we do.

Being Helpful

We believe in helping our coworkers and going above and beyond for animals.

Accountability

We believe in taking responsibility for mistakes, learning from them, and making things right.

Our Community Partners

We are thankful for local veterinary clinics, animal welfare organizations, and community partners that help us care for animals and find them loving homes.
