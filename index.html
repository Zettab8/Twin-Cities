Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a nonprofit animal shelter dedicated to saving animals in need. We are always ready to help, whether an animal is a stray or a pet being surrendered by its owner.

Our Impact

Last year, 5,100 animals found their forever homes.

23% of our dogs and cats were transferred from overloaded shelters.

How You Can Help

Any amount of help can make a difference. We encourage anyone with extra time, even during a lunch break, to spend time walking one of our dogs. Something as simple as a walk can make a big impact on an animal's life. We are always grateful for an extra set of hands.
